Privacy Policy

MajorMap · Effective September 22, 2026
The short version: MajorMap has no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no server of its own. It collects nothing about you. The only network requests it makes are to the U.S. Department of Education's College Scorecard API to fetch the list of colleges you search for.

What MajorMap is

MajorMap is a free, open-source web app (and installable progressive web app) published by PVG Production. It helps you find U.S. college majors, the colleges in a state that offer them, a course roadmap by degree level, and the professional organizations connected to each field.

Information we collect

None. MajorMap does not ask for, store, or transmit your name, email address, location, or any other personal information. There is no sign-up and no login. We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising networks, or third-party cookies.

Network requests the app makes

  • College Scorecard API (api.data.gov), operated by the U.S. Department of Education. When you search, the app sends the state and program code you chose so it can return matching colleges. Your IP address is visible to that service as with any web request; its handling is governed by the U.S. Department of Education privacy policy and api.data.gov's privacy policy.
  • Hosting: the app is served from GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web-server data (such as IP address and user agent) under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

No other requests are made. Links to professional organizations open those organizations' own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Data stored on your device

MajorMap may store a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, only on your device, and never sent to us:

  • An optional College Scorecard API key, if you choose to enter your own (sent only to api.data.gov with your searches).
  • Whether you've dismissed the "install" or "add to home screen" hint.
  • Cached copies of the app's own files so it loads faster and works offline (via a service worker).

You can clear all of it at any time by clearing site data for this site in your browser settings or by uninstalling the app.

Children's privacy

MajorMap is a general-audience reference tool. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.
